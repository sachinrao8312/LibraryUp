## Full description (EN)
Run your self-study library or reading room from your phone. No register, no Excel, no message credits.

**Seat map by shift**: see free seats in the morning, afternoon, evening or full-day shift at a glance. Green is free, red is full, orange means fee due.

**Fees made simple**
• Monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, yearly or day plans
• Calendar-month or 30-day fee cycle
• Partial payments, discount and late fee
• One numbered PDF receipt per payment, shared on WhatsApp
• Dashboard: collected today, this month, overdue, due and expiring

**WhatsApp reminders, unlimited and free**: fee-due and renewal reminders go from your own WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business, in Hindi or English, with a UPI pay link. No SMS packs, no message limits.

**Students**: photo, ID proof, father's name, address, exam, guardian phone. Renew in one tap, change seat or shift, freeze while a student is away, mark left.

**Attendance**: In and Out time for every student, every day.

**Expenses and profit**: rent, electricity, Wi-Fi, salary, with monthly profit.

**Your data stays yours**: your students' details stay on your phone, with optional daily backup to *your own* Google Drive, encrypted with your password. LibraryUp has no server holding your students' data.

**Import and export**: import students from Excel (CSV); export students and payments any time, free.

Hindi and English. Made in India for self-study libraries, reading rooms and study centres.
